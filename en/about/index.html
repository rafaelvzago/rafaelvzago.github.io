Qué pasa?

Hi! Don’t take me too seriously, okay? I built this space to share some of what I know — and about 90% of it came from the internet. So it was past time to give a little back.

Today I’m a senior software automation engineer at Red Hat. Before that I bounced through small and large companies doing a bit of everything:

  • DevOps at heart, sysadmin by vocation.
  • Designing learning methods and structuring training.
  • Years of application support across sizes and complexity.
  • Instructor at Caelum/Alura.
  • Godfather to the coolest kid on earth. No clubism…
