Introduction

Qué pasa?

Hi. Don’t take me too seriously, okay? I built this space to share a bit of what I know—and about 90% of it came from the internet. So it was past time to give some of it back.

Today I’m a senior software automation engineer at Red Hat. Before that I bounced through a few companies, small and large, doing a bit of everything:

  • DevOps at heart, sysadmin by vocation.
  • Building learning methods and structuring training.
  • Years of application support across all sizes and complexity levels.
  • Instructor at Caelum/Alura.
  • Godfather to the coolest kid on the planet. No bias here…
